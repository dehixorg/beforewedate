# BeforeWeMeet - The Complete Vision

## Core Problem
In early dating, sharing interest too early creates pressure, platforms collect too much private data, and matching is shallow. Users want emotional support, safety, and better first-conversation guidance.

## Core Solution (The MVP)
A consent-first A2A compatibility agent that lets two people privately evaluate compatibility before identities are revealed.
- **Swipe / Discovery:** Standard interface but completely private matching.
- **Consent Policy Manager:** Choose what can be used for matching, revealed after match, or kept completely private.
- **Compatibility Agent (CROO CAP):** Paid callable agent that deterministically evaluates consent and matching alignment.
- **Anonymous Invitation Flow:** Target user is only informed if compatibility passes. Identities are hidden until acceptance.
- **Serendipity Map (Crossed Paths):** Map-like serendipity for profiles you crossed paths with.
- **Limited Compliments:** Premium feature to prioritize a note.
- **Daily Blind Chat:** Once a day, the agent matches you with the most compatible person for a 30-min anonymous chat to discuss life/love. No names/numbers shared until both accept.

## Long-Term Vision (Post-MVP)
This platform goes beyond dating—it acts as a life-long privacy and relationship manager.

### Physical Safety & India-Specific Features
- **Trust Scores & Identity Verification:** Selfie/ID checks for safer dating.
- **Number Masking:** Absolute privacy before revealing identity.
- **Uber MCP Integration:** Automatically order safe rides to verified venues.
- **Designated Restaurants:** Partnerships for safe, verified dating spots.
- **SOS & Friends on Standby Mode:** Live location sharing and check-ins with a "standby" friend circle during the first date.

### Progression Modes
The agent evolves as the relationship grows:
1. **Dating Mode:** The MVP matching and compatibility features.
2. **Couple Mode:** Once a relationship is established, the app switches to helping maintain the relationship (date night suggestions, conflict resolution coach).
3. **Marriage / Memory Mode:** Storing memories, major milestones, and life building.
4. **Child Care & Support Mode:** Connecting all relevant MCPs for childcare, family planning, and household management.
